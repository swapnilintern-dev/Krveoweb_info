How we work

Principles we
actually operate on

01

Say what we have

Stock positions are shared honestly. If a line is short, you hear it before you commit to a customer.

02

Price without games

Trade rates are structured and explained. No mystery discounts that reappear as costs elsewhere.

03

Answer quickly

Quotes and stock queries get a same-day reply during working hours, on whichever channel you used.

04

Ship it properly

Packing protects the goods and the paperwork matches the carton. Fewer disputes, faster reconciliation.

05

Own the problem

Damaged or incorrect goods are resolved against written terms, not left to wear down over email.

06

Grow with partners

Ranges and reorder levels are adjusted around what our partners sell season after season.
