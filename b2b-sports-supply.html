Bulk supply

What a bulk order
can include

One requirement can pull from any part of the 15+-category catalogue — you do not need a separate vendor for apparel, footwear, equipment and infrastructure.

Equipment in quantity

Balls, bats, rackets, nets, goals, stumps, cones, hurdles, ladders and weights in the counts a squad or a shelf needs.

Team & uniform sets

Jerseys, shorts, tracksuits and team uniforms in size runs — including house-wise and squad-wise sets.

Protective & safety gear

Helmets, guards, pads and gloves for contact sports, coaching sessions and match play.

Accessories & consumables

Bags, bottles, grips, tapes, pumps, whistles and stopwatches — the lines that reorder most often.

Tournament supplies

Bibs, number plates, referee equipment, scoreboards, timing equipment, medals, trophies and certificates.

Infrastructure

Courts, surfaces, turf, flooring, lighting, seating and fencing, scoped as a project alongside the equipment order.
